# Official Sources and Dated Baseline

The links below are the source map for live checks. The baseline is a dated
change detector, not a substitute for opening the current official source.

## Source priority

1. **Binding law:** current EUR-Lex consolidated text plus every authentic
   amending act in the Official Journal.
2. **Authoritative interpretation:** final Commission Guidelines, official FAQ,
   Commission/AI Board assessments, and AI Act Service Desk materials.
3. **Voluntary compliance framework:** current final Code of Practice for
   organisations that rely on or sign it.
4. **Official technical material:** EU icon page and archives, Commission
   technical studies, current standards/specifications from their owner.
5. **National implementation:** current official legislation registers,
   government or ministry material, and designated-authority material for each
   named Member State.
